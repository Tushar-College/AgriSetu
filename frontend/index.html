Data Transparency & Standards

How AgriSetu Works

An open, transparent guide to where our data comes from, what our labels mean, and our system boundaries.

1. What This Platform Is and Is Not

✅ What AgriSetu Is:

  • An information-access tool for Indian farmers.
  • A neutral reference to compare buyer offers against recent reported market prices.
  • A curated directory of potentially relevant government schemes.
  • A mobile-first tool designed for speed and clarity in rural connectivity.

❌ What AgriSetu Is NOT:

  • NOT a marketplace or trading platform.
  • NOT a buying, selling, or payment system.
  • NOT a fraud detector or buyer accusation system.
  • NOT a guaranteed or authoritative price system.
  • NOT an official eligibility determination service.

2. Understanding Our Data Classifications

[REPORTED]

Data directly pulled from official wholesale mandi records via the government Agmarknet / data.gov.in portal. Represents actual arrivals and transactions recorded by market committees.

[FALLBACK / PRE-TESTED DEMO DATA]

Pre-tested benchmark records used when live government APIs are unreachable or offline during network disruptions. Clearly marked so users are never misled.

[CALCULATED]

Any arithmetic computed by this application, such as unit conversions (quintal to kg), offer position checks (Above / Within / Below), or quantity totals. Never disguised as reported raw data.

[POTENTIALLY RELEVANT]

Applied to every government scheme. We never say "You are eligible" because only government authorities can verify land titles and eligibility conditions.

3. Why We Never Say "Fair Price" or "Correct Price"

Agricultural pricing in real-world mandis is subject to multiple factors beyond geographical averages, including:

  • Crop Variety & Grade: High-grade export quality commands different prices than local grade.
  • Moisture & Freshness: Storage conditions, sorting, and freshness directly influence offer prices.
  • Transportation & Packaging: Distance to market, crate packaging, and loading costs impact net realizations.
  • Intraday Volatility: Mandi prices fluctuate daily or hourly depending on arrival truck volumes.

Therefore, this platform remains strictly neutral. We provide the recent reported market range so that farmers enter negotiations informed, without asserting an absolute single "correct" price.

Indicative Reference Disclaimer: All market price information displayed on this platform is for general informational and reference purposes only. It does not constitute a legal, guaranteed, or binding commercial quote.
